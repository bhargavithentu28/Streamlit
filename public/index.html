⚡ Core Equipment Categories

The model identifies high-voltage distribution and transmission apparatus from optical photographs:

  • 🔹 Transformer: Power/distribution units, radiators, and primary bushings.
  • 🔹 Circuit Breaker: SF6, vacuum, and air-blast high-voltage interruptors.
  • 🔹 Insulator: Ceramic, porcelain, and composite suspension disc strings.
  • 🔹 Disconnect Switch: Air-break isolators and rotating bus switches.
  • 🔹 Current Transformer (CT): Toroidal instrument transformers for current sensing.
  • 🔹 Voltage Transformer (VT/PT): Potential transformers for voltage measurement.

📐 Pipeline Specifications

  • 🔸 Resolution & Color: Images resized to 128 × 128 pixels in 3-channel RGB format.
  • 🔸 Pixel Normalization: Raw [0, 255] pixels scaled strictly to [0.0, 1.0] via 1.0 / 255.0.
  • 🔸 Data Leakage Prevention: Perceptual MD5 hashing detects exact and near-duplicates to ensure zero leakage across splits.
  • 🔸 Training-Only Augmentation: Random horizontal flip, ±10% rotation, ±10% zoom, and contrast adjustment.
  • 🔸 Validation/Test Invariance: Deterministic resizing and scaling, strictly zero augmentation applied.

About the EEIC Project

Engineering context, neural architecture deep dive, and operational boundaries of the electrical equipment classification system.

🎯 Problem Statement

In high-voltage electrical substations, automated recognition of apparatus is critical for digital twin inventory, drone inspection sorting, and maintenance logging. Manual photo sorting is slow and error-prone across hundreds of substations.

🧠 CNN Backbone Architecture

The model uses a multi-block convolutional neural network optimized for CPU and edge inference:

  • 1. Block 1: Conv2D (32, 3x3) + BatchNorm + Conv2D (32, 3x3) + MaxPool + Dropout(0.20)
  • 2. Block 2: Conv2D (64, 3x3) + BatchNorm + Conv2D (64, 3x3) + MaxPool + Dropout(0.25)
  • 3. Block 3: Conv2D (128, 3x3) + BatchNorm + MaxPool + Dropout(0.30)
  • 4. Block 4: Conv2D (256, 3x3) + BatchNorm + MaxPool + Dropout(0.35)
  • 5. Head: GlobalAveragePooling2D + Dense(128, ReLU) + Dropout(0.35) + Dense(6, Softmax)

⚠️ Safety & Engineering Boundaries

Important Safety Notice: This model is an equipment category classifier. It does not certify dielectric integrity, measure insulation degradation, or confirm de-energized status. Never use automated computer vision alone for electrical safety clearance or lockout/tagout (LOTO) protocols.
